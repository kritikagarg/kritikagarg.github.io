Experience

Research and engineering roles across a university lab, a nonprofit digital library and an industry research group.

  • Present

    Doctoral Researcher · Web Science and Digital Libraries Research Group, Old Dominion University

    • Dissertation: Conducted a longitudinal study of web page lifespans and evolution using a sample of 27.3 million URLs and 3.8 billion Wayback Machine mementos (1996–2021). Proposed the Multi-Resident URL framework to distinguish URL persistence from content identity, and characterized redirection behavior across 11 million chains and 2.6 million domains, observing a 50% end-to-end failure rate.
    • Web archiving: Co-authored studies showing that robots continue to outnumber human users of web archives (TPDL 2022 Best Student Paper; IJDL 2024); proposed caching HTTP 404 responses to eliminate recurrent replay requests (ICADL 2022); and documented replay failures, missing content labels and temporal violations in archived Twitter pages (JCDL 2021; IJDL 2023).
    • News analytics: Developed BigQuery pipelines over millions of GDELT news records to quantify sentiment trends and coverage gaps concerning marginalized communities for the “What’s Missing?” project.
