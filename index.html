Kritika Garg

Researcher · Data Scientist · Educator

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at Old Dominion University, advised by Michael L. Nelson and Michele C. Weigle. My research measures how the web changes and decays over time, drawing on billions of archived snapshots from the Internet Archive’s Wayback Machine.

I also build large language model systems, from GPU inference and retrieval-augmented generation on Kubernetes to a merged contribution to an open-source Mixture-of-Experts engine. I previously worked as an NLP researcher at IBM and a software engineer at the Internet Archive, and I have taught my own course in web security.

I am seeking postdoctoral and faculty positions, as well as research scientist and applied ML roles in industry.

  • Web science & measurement
  • Information retrieval & NLP
